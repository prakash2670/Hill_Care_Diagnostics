What we do

We offer 18+ categories of lab tests and health packages: Full Body Checkup, Diabetes, Thyroid, Heart Health, Liver, Kidney, Vitamin D & B12, Bone & Calcium, Prostate PSA, Gastro, Pregnancy, PCOD, Cervix, Allergy, Anemia, TB and Fever tests.
